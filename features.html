EVERY FEATURE, EXPLAINED

What's actually under the hood.

Same engine running Papers & Petals across 3 branches. Here's what it does — and what you'd get if I set it up for your shop.

01 / INVENTORY

Stock that's actually accurate, per branch.

Every branch sees its own levels. No more calling around to check if something's on hand.

LiveStock sync across branches
✓
Per-branch stock levels
Tracked independently — North Plaza having 120 doesn't mean Midtown can sell it.
✓
Safe stock adjustments
Sales, edits, and restocks update the count without two people taking the last unit twice.
✓
Daily inventory counts
Staff submit actuals. Reports stay locked until the count is in — so the day isn't fiction.
✓
Printable stock sheets
Thermal / print-ready lists for counting, shift handover, or the rider's packing slip.
02 / BRANCH TRANSFERS

Move stock between branches without losing track.

When one branch is drowning and another is empty, the move is requested, logged, and printable — not a phone call.

✓
Request & approve flow
A branch asks, admin approves. Stock doesn't vanish in a group chat.
✓
Line-item transfers
Multiple products in one request, each counted individually.
✓
Full movement history
Every move is logged — useful for disputes, patterns, and “where did this go?”
03 / ORDERS & COUNTER

Walk-in or scheduled — logged, not buried in chat.

Staff ring up a walk-in or take a pickup for Saturday. Same record: items, photos, money owed, status you can trust.

✓
Full order lifecycle
Not started → ready → picked up / delivered. You're not rewriting a Messenger thread to match reality.
✓
Stock deducted automatically
A sale or an edit updates inventory. No separate “minus 12 stems” step.
✓
Photos & notes on the order
Inspiration, proof of payment, finished photo — sitting on the ticket, not in someone's camera roll.
✓
Thermal slips
Order tickets and packing lists for the wrapping table and the rider.
04 / PAYMENTS

Half now, rest later — recorded how it actually happened.

Cash down, GCash for the balance, a bank transfer at another branch. One “paid” checkbox is how reports start lying.

✓
Downpayment vs remaining
Collecting the rest is an explicit step. Reports don't assume they probably paid.
✓
Methods per branch
Cash, GCash, card terminal, bank rails — on or off depending on how that location actually banks.
05 / STAFF & PERMISSIONS

Not everyone needs to see everything.

Owners, co-admins, and branch staff each see what's relevant — including hiding cost and net sales if that's how the family wants it.

✓
Admin & co-admin
Co-admin runs the day. COGS and net stay with the owner.
✓
Branch-scoped staff
Tied to their branch. No shared Facebook login, no other location's books.
✓
Guided first login
New hires get an onboarding step, not a blank dashboard and a group-chat password.
06 / REPORTS

Know the number without doing the math yourself.

Sales, cash on hand, expenses, cost — by branch or combined, with the right figures hidden from the people who shouldn't see them.

✓
Daily & monthly
Revenue and the private math, by period and by branch.
✓
Role-based visibility
Staff and co-admin never “accidentally” open COGS.
✓
Printable recaps
A sheet for the folder, the accountant, or close-of-day.
07 / SUPPLIES & EXPENSES

The costs behind the sales, tracked too.

Hub buys, branches receive, petty cash gets logged. “Net” actually means something.

✓
Hub → branch supplies
Vouchers and liability when the main location pushes stock out. Not a photo of a receipt in chat.
✓
Staff expenses vs supplier cost
A tricycle run isn't inventory. Both feed reports, separately.
